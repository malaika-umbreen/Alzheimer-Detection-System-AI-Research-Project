Alzheimer's Disease

Alzheimer's disease involves parts of the brain that control thought, memory, and language.

About Us

Alzheimer's Disease is a progressive neurological disorder that affects millions of individuals worldwide, often beginning with subtle memory loss and gradually impacting a person’s ability to communicate and respond to their surroundings. This disease, the most common type of dementia, affects crucial areas of the brain responsible for thought, memory, and language. As Alzheimer's advances, it imposes significant challenges on both individuals and their families. Our website aims to support early and accurate detection of Alzheimer's disease by analyzing MRI images. Using advanced machine learning models, our system classifies MRI images into one of four stages: Non Demented: No signs of dementia are detected. Very Mild Demented: Initial symptoms appear, with minimal impact on daily activities. Mild Demented: Noticeable symptoms affect memory and thinking but are manageable with support. Moderate Demented: Advanced symptoms that significantly affect day-to-day life. Our goal is to empower individuals and healthcare providers with a tool that aids in identifying Alzheimer's disease stages. Early detection can be crucial for planning and managing care, allowing individuals and their families to make informed decisions about treatment and lifestyle adjustments.
